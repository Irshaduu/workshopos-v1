The Shop Price is the shop's answer (blank waiting, 0 free, an amount paid). Nothing is added or removed here — a wrong claim is cancelled from the ⋮, and anything not from the earlier bill goes on a job card and is billed. The rare replacement taken from Unassigned Spares is filled into this one row from a quiet link under it; * Work done, then photos and a note. No customer box, no customer price, no labour charge — the customer pays nothing, and `JobCard.save()` holds that whatever is posted. Every link that opens a warranty card lands here through `jobcard_edit`'s redirect. The Complaint and Work done rows are the job card's own machinery (script.js: `addFormRow` and the suggestions), so those two lists keep the job card's ids. ========================================================================== {% endcomment %}
