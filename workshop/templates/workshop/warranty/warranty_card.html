ONE STYLESHEET, NOT A COPY. This template EXTENDS `jobcard/jobcard_form.html` and overrides only the title and the content, so the job card's whole `extra_css` block (and its date chip) is inherited as it stands. A second copy of those rules would drift from the first the day either is touched — the `.rpay-*` rule. ⚠ Anything added to the job card's `extra_css` reaches this page too; that is the point. Top to bottom: * the header — WR number, the car, the part and which claim, the bill it is FOR and how old the warranty is, the Slip (Office and Owner) and a ⋮; * Vehicle Details — this visit's date, mileage and mechanic (and the make and model, only while the earlier bill left them blank); * Workshop Note & Photos — the job card's fold, as Floor's job card draws it (there is no customer here to name); * Customer Concerns and Job Performed — the job card's own rows; * Claimed Part — ONE CLAIM IS ONE PART: the part from the earlier bill as the job card's spare row (or its inventory row), with no customer price. The Shop Price is the shop's answer (blank waiting, 0 free, an amount paid). Nothing is added or removed here — a wrong claim is cancelled from the ⋮, and anything not from the earlier bill goes on a job card and is billed. No customer box, no customer price, no labour charge — the customer pays nothing, and `JobCard.save()` holds that whatever is posted. Every link that opens a warranty card lands here through `jobcard_edit`'s redirect. ========================================================================== {% endcomment %} {% block content %}
