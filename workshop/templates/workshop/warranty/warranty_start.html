{% extends 'workshop/base.html' %} {% block title %}Warranty · {{ registration }}{% endblock %} {% block content %} {% comment %} ========================================================================== THE CAR'S WARRANTY PAGE — which part failed? ========================================================================== Reached from the Car Profile's Warranty button, or from New claim's car list. Every rule is `workshop/warranty.py`. The owners' flow: a customer says "the coolant elbow broke — is it under warranty?". Find it here (the search box), read how long ago its bill was — whether it is still covered is the owner's call, the system never decides it — and press Claim on that part. ONE CLAIM IS ONE PART. This car's bills, newest first — finished job cards, warranty cards (a replacement fitted under warranty can fail too) and Excel-era old bills — one block each, with its date, how long ago, and an Open link to the bill. ONE DATE RULE: the date under a bill's number is that bill's own date. A warranty card's block is tinted and reads "20 Aug 2026 · 2nd claim · for WR-26-001" ("2nd claim" only for a repeat — the first needs no word); its age is not the warranty clock, because a claim never restarts it (`warranty.first_bill`), so the clock sits on the part you would claim, beside its button: "First fitted JB-26-043 · 8 months 26 days". A claim's chain is spread over the page, so each of those numbers, and "Replaced · WR-…", jumps to that bill's block here. Under each block, its parts, each saying where it stands: * a Claim button — it can be claimed; one question, then its card opens; * "Being claimed · WR-…" — a claim is open on it (a link to that card); * "Replaced · WR-…" — a claim on it is finished: the new part, listed under that WR bill (the link jumps to it), is the one on the car now, and a failure is claimed THERE; * "Newer one on JB-…" (grey, beside the button) — the same part was fitted again on a later bill. A note, never a block. A bill's STOCK parts fold under "Stock items · N" (a part off our own shelf is rarely claimed); a bill with only stock parts shows them open, and an old bill never split the two. There is no claim for the work alone (the owners, 2026-10-08): a fix with no part is done without a card. No script is needed to claim: every button is its own small form, and the server holds every rule. The search box filters as you type. ========================================================================== {% endcomment %}
